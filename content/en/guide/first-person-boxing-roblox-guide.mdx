## What First Person Boxing Actually Is

First Person Boxing is a Roblox fighting game where you box from the first person view the genre is named for. You create a fighter, queue into matches against other players or AI, and earn spins, coins and cash for every win. The hook is the style system: rather than one fixed moveset, your fighter's punches, speed and special ability are defined by which fighting style you have equipped — and styles are unlocked through spins, the game's gacha currency. ## Controls and the Basics of a Fight

The control scheme is simple to learn and hard to master:

- **Movement** uses standard WASD; footwork is half the game because range decides exchanges.
- **Punches** are bound to left and right mouse buttons for jabs and straights, with hooks and uppercuts on keyboard modifiers.
- **Block** holds a guard that absorbs chip damage — blocking everything drains stamina and eventually breaks.
- **Dodge** is a directional slip on a double-tap, the highest-skill defensive tool in the game.

The core exchange loop: jab to control entry, slip the counter, then commit to power shots when the opponent's guard is low. New players lose almost every fight by swinging heavies first — the tutorial-style advice is to survive one full round just blocking and moving before you try to win one.

## Movement: The Skill Nobody Teaches

Footwork is the quiet difference between every bracket in First Person Boxing. Three habits separate climbing movement from stuck movement:

**Never stand square after a combination.** Land your string, then step off the center line. Opponents pre-aim their counter where you finished punching, not where you are two beats later.

**Use the edge of your range, not the middle.** Every style has a distance where its jab connects and the opponent's does not. Find it in the first thirty seconds of any fight and live there. Half of all knockout losses in lobby play happen because a fighter drifted two steps too close while swinging.

**Punish forward movement with timing, not retreat.** Backpedaling fighters lose to pressure eventually. The counter is a slip-and-step: dodge the entry punch and move diagonally forward, trading places with the aggressor. It is the single highest-value mechanic to drill in the lobby before ranked placement.
